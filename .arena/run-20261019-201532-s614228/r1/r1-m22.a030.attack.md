ATTACK 2 [FATAL] Broken Predication Formula Reverses Inactive vs Active Threads and Mismatches Ragged Tail Example
Where: Section 4.2 line 71:
`$$\text{Predication Flag } @p0 = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } \text{ThreadIdx.x} < (seq\_len \pmod{32}) \\ 0 & \text{if } \text{ThreadIdx.x} \ge (seq\_len \pmod{32}) \end{cases}$$`
and Section 3.1 line 49:
`The Sequence Length Scrubber is primed at 1023 tokens—a deliberate non-multiple of 32 that triggers tail-warp divergence.`
Problem: Factual and mathematical error in GPU SIMT predication semantics. $1023 \pmod{32} \equiv 31$. Under a sequence length of 1023 tokens, 1023 elements are processed across warps; in the tail warp (covering indices 992 to 1023), exactly 31 elements exist (threads 0 through 30), meaning only 1 lane is out of bounds (thread 31). However, the formula defines:
$\text{ThreadIdx.x} \ge (seq\_len \pmod{32}) \implies \text{ThreadIdx.x} \ge 31 \implies @p0 = 0$.
More critically, if $seq\_len$ is an exact multiple of 32 (e.g., $1024$), $1024 \pmod{32} = 0$. Applying the formula yields:
$\forall \text{ThreadIdx.x} \ge 0, @p0 = 0$, meaning every single thread in every warp evaluates to inactive ($@p0 = 0$) when the sequence is perfectly aligned! This completely breaks hardware simulation for aligned workloads and inverts predication logic for tail blocks.
