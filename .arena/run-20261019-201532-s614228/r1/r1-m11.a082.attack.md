Problem: The proposal mounts `<InteractiveBoundaryScrubber seqLen={seqLen} onChange={(val) => setSeqLen(val)} isRunning={isRunning} />` directly inside Tab 1 (`collider`). ATTACK 5 [MINOR] Redundant Double Audio Invocation Triggers Audio Engine Glitches
Where: `frontend/src/components/InteractiveBoundaryScrubber.jsx` line 184 (`sound.playClick()`) and `ExecutiveCommandBar.jsx` / `App.jsx`
Problem: The standalone `InteractiveBoundaryScrubber` imports and directly fires `sound.playClick()` inside its preset handlers without respecting the global mute state (`isMuted`) tracked in `ExecutiveCommandBar.jsx`.
