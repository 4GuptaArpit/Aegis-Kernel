ATTACK 1 [FATAL] Client-side fallback telemetry is completely absent despite explicit claim, leaving offline or un-mocked users permanently broken
Where: Section 2 Assumptions ("When the backend is offline or simulating, client-side fallback telemetry ensures smooth interactive demonstrations") & Section 5.1 App.jsx `handleLaunchProvingGround`
Problem: The architecture explicitly asserts that client-side fallback telemetry ensures smooth interactive demonstrations when backend is offline. However, in `App.jsx`, `eventSource.onerror` solely executes `setIsRunning(false); eventSource.close();` without supplying mock events, fallback data, or fallback telemetry. If the backend at `http://localhost:8000` is offline or unreachable, clicking "Engage Proving Collider" immediately aborts into silence with zero fallback telemetry rendered, completely breaking interactive evaluation by judges.

Where: Section 5.1 App.jsx `<WarpVisualizer selectedFixture={selectedFixture} isRunning={isRunning} currentStage={currentStage} />` & Section 5.3 WarpVisualizer.jsx
Problem: Requirement 5 explicitly demands "dynamic sequence length scrubber ... and live execution proving loops", and Section 7 claims "Dynamic sequence length parameter adjustment ($N \in [1, 4096]$) cleanly differentiates aligned ($N \pmod{32} = 0$) and ragged ($N \pmod{32} \ne 0$) tensor boundaries". ATTACK 4 [MAJOR] Hopper die GPC cluster math creates an invalid 9th GPC (GPC 0 through 8) instead of 8 GPCs
Where: Section 5.2 HopperDieTopology.jsx `title={`Streaming Multiprocessor SM-${idx} (GPC ${Math.floor(idx / 17)})`}`
Problem: Requirement 4 demands authentic representation of NVIDIA Hopper GH100 architecture ("132 Streaming Multiprocessors, ... 8 GPCs"). For `idx` from 119 to 131, `Math.floor(idx / 17)` evaluates to `7`, but for `idx = 132` (or if 136 SMs are present), or specifically at `idx = 136`, `136 / 17 = 8`, which indexes an impossible 9th GPC (GPC 8 in 0-indexed notation). More critically, GH100 features 8 GPCs with up to 18 SMs per full GPC (or 16-17 active SMs per GPC in SXM5). Dividing by 17 uniformly puts only 17 SMs in GPCs 0-6 (119 SMs) and leaves 13 SMs in GPC 7, misrepresenting the physical cluster architecture of Hopper silicon.
