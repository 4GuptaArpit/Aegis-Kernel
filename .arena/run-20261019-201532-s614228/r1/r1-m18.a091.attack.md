Problem: Misses Requirement 4: "Hardware Authenticity: Must authentically represent NVIDIA Hopper GH100 architecture". Stating that the GH100 silicon die is flanked by 4 HBM3e stacks is factually incorrect GPU architecture modeling that breaks authentic Hopper representation.
