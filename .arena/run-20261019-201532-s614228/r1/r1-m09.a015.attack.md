ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] Inverted conditional styling in WarpVisualizer produces identical text colors for vulnerable and hardened states
Where: `frontend/src/components/WarpVisualizer.jsx#L487-L489`
Problem: In `WarpVisualizer.jsx`, the predicate register HUD indicator attempts to color-code based on whether the lane is valid, vulnerable, or hardened. However, the ternary logic is implemented as:
`className={selectedThreadInfo.isValid ? 'text-nvidia font-bold' : (effectiveMode === 'vulnerable' ? 'text-coral font-bold' : 'text-nvidia font-bold')}`.
When a masked lane is inspected in hardened mode (`isValid === false` and `effectiveMode === 'hardened'`), it falls back to the third branch: `'text-nvidia font-bold'`. But the first branch (`isValid === true`) also applies `'text-nvidia font-bold'`. As a result, active executing threads (`@p0 = 1`) and masked/suppressed threads (`@p0 = 0`) render in the exact same green color, failing to visually distinguish active hardware compute lanes from predicated-off lanes in the telemetry HUD.

ATTACK 4 [MAJOR] Critical hardware microarchitecture errors: GH100 memory configuration and Blackwell B200 SM counts are factually wrong
Where: Section 1 Assumptions, item 1 (`r0/a018.md#L11`) and Section 6.3 (`r0/a018.md#L527`)
Problem: Requirement 4 demands "Hardware Authenticity: Must authentically represent NVIDIA Hopper GH100 architecture (132 Streaming Multiprocessors... 4-tier memory hierarchy)". Section 1 claims: "NVIDIA Hopper GH100 SXM5 (primary: 132 SMs, 8 GPCs, 4 HBM3e stacks @ 3.35 TB/s, SM_90a), NVIDIA Blackwell B200 (160 SMs, 8.0 TB/s, SM_100)". This is factually erroneous on two counts:
1. NVIDIA Hopper GH100 SXM5 utilizes 5 active stacks of HBM3 (out of 6 on package) providing 80 GB at 3.35 TB/s, not "4 HBM3e stacks". (HBM3e was introduced on the H200 with 6 active stacks / 141 GB).
2. NVIDIA Blackwell B200 features a dual-die package with 160 SMs enabled per die (148 to 160 active SMs per die, totaling up to 192-208 SMs across the full dual-die module), not a flat 160 SMs total.
Presenting 4 stacks of HBM3e for GH100 SXM5 to NVIDIA hardware evaluators undermines the core claim of authentic silicon microarchitecture modeling.
