# Draft 1: Micro-Instrument HUD
Dense, multi-pane single cockpit with 4 quarter-screens, high informational density, minimized spacing.
Pros: All metrics visible at once.
Cons: High visual clutter, violates the non-congested breathing room mandate.

# Draft 2: Stepped Wizard Carousel
Linear sequential progression through verification steps.
Pros: Focused attention, very clean.
Cons: Loses the interactive supercomputer workbench feeling where engineers can jump between SM topology and PTX disassembly at will.

# Draft 3: Autonomous Cyber-Physical Spatial Supercomputer Proving Architecture (Chosen)
A multi-tier hierarchical workbench featuring:
1. Executive Command Cockpit (Hardware target switcher, workload presets, dynamic sequence length scrubber, audio synthesis engine, and real-time 5-stage laser pipeline stepper).
2. 4-Stage Ergonomic Spatial Navigator with Stage Indicators & Micro-Telemetry Badges:
   - Stage 01: Red vs Blue Collider & Empirical Mic-Drop Delta
   - Stage 02: 132-SM Silicon Die Topology & 32-Warp SIMT Matrix
   - Stage 03: AST Diff & Hopper PTX ISA Disassembly (@p0 predication)
   - Stage 04: Nsight Compute Telemetry HUD & 4-Tier Memory Hierarchy Waterfall
3. Deep architectural hardware fidelity: TSMC 4N, 8 GPCs, 132 SMs, 32 thread lanes, 50MB L2 crossbar, 4 HBM3e stacks (3.35 TB/s), 228KB SRAM/SM, 4th Gen Tensor Cores.
4. Generous breathing room, 1580px structured grid layout, zero cramped card stacks, obsidian void `#03060D` with laser emerald `#00FF88` and plasma coral `#FF0055`.
